# Design context — Framesmith

## Audience
Indie makers, founders, devrel folks and designers who post product screenshots on X, LinkedIn,
Product Hunt, changelogs and docs several times a week. Today they pay for Xnapper, Shots.so or
CleanShot X (≈$5–$29/mo) to make a raw screenshot look presentable.

## Jobs
1. Paste a screenshot → get a share-ready image in under ten seconds.
2. Point at something (arrow, box, numbered steps) and hide something (redact) before posting.
3. Hit the exact size a platform wants (16:9 for X, 1.91:1 for Open Graph, 4:5 for feeds).
4. Copy to clipboard and paste straight into the composer.

## Personality
A craftsperson's workbench, not a SaaS dashboard. Warm paper, ink, one hot vermilion accent.
Calm, precise, tactile; the screenshot is the hero and the chrome gets out of the way.
Typography: Instrument Serif for the wordmark and section numerals, Geist for UI.

## Principles
- The canvas is always the loudest thing on screen.
- Zero accounts, zero uploads: everything runs in the browser. Say so, once, quietly.
- Every control previews live; nothing needs an "apply" button.
- Keyboard-first for power users (V A R H T N B F, ⌘C, ⌘S, ⌘Z), obvious for everyone else.
